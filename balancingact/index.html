How to play

  1. Hang every item from the store on a plate, until every beam hangs level.
  2. A beam is level when weight × arm is the same on both sides. The numbers on a beam are its arms' lengths, and a side weighs everything hanging under it.
  3. Drag an item onto a plate, or click it and then click a plate. Drag it back to the store to take it off.
  4. Drag an item onto a plate, or tap it and then tap a plate. Drag it back to the store to take it off.
  5. A balloon lifts: a −2 balloon cancels out a 2 weight. Some come tied to their plate with a bow.
  6. Use every item. There's only one way.
  7. Every drop counts as a move, and Restart doesn't undo them: within one move of the fewest for three stars.

Keyboard: arrows pick an item, 1–8 hang it on that plate, 0 back to the store, P pause.
