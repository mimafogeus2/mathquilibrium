Today's zine

Print the zine

All of today's printable puzzles in one little booklet, answers at the back. Print it, fold it, staple it, and solve with a pen.

 

  1. Print on A4, one side only, at 100% scale. Each sheet holds two pages.
  2. Fold each sheet in half along the dashed line, printed side out, so its left page is the front.
  3. Stack the folded sheets in order, page 1 on top, and staple twice along the open edge.
  1. Print on A4, both sides, at 100% scale. Choose flip on short edge.
  2. Stack the sheets in order, sheet 1 on the outside, and fold them in half together, page 1 on the front.
  3. Staple twice through the fold, from the outside. Opening the stapler flat onto some cardboard helps.
