How to play

catourainicegg

  1. Link every word in the box into one long word. The chain starts with a word of its own.
  2. Type a word that starts with the chain's last letter and ends with the first letter of a word in the box. The computer then adds that word, and it's your turn again.
  1. Your words can't be words from the box, and each can be used once. Harder levels ask for longer ones.
  2. If two words in the box start with the same letter, the computer picks one that still lets you finish. Your choices can still lead you into a dead end: Undo takes back your last word and the one it brought in, as often as you like.
  1. Stars are for speed. One hint a level fills in a word for you (backing out of a dead end first), and costs a star.

Word lists: SCOWL and FrequencyWords (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Keyboard: type, Enter to add your word, Backspace to delete, Ctrl+Z undo, Esc menu.
