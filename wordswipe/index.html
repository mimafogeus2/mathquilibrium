How to play

  1. Each round has a subject and 24 words.
  2. Swipe right (or →) if the word fits the subject, left (or ←) if it doesn't.
  3. You have 60 seconds. A wrong swipe costs 3 seconds. Run out and it's game over.
  4. 10 points per right answer, plus 5 points for every second left when you finish.

One daily round, the same for everyone, plus unlimited rounds.
