Orderly

Ten cards, one subject. Put them in order.

Daily

    Levels

    How to play

    1. Each puzzle has a subject and 10 cards.
    2. Drag the cards into order, top to bottom. Keyboard: focus a card and move it with ↑ ↓.
    3. Press Check. Cards that sit next to each other in the right order turn green, show their value, and join into a group — even if the group isn't in its final spot yet. Drag a group to move it as one.
    4. Solve it in 2 checks for three stars, 4 for two.

    One daily puzzle, the same for everyone, plus a long run of levels.
