How to play

  1. Each level shows 10 pairs of sums, without their answers.
  2. Tap the bigger one, or press ← / →.
  3. The clock runs: 30s on Easy, 45s on Medium, 60s on Hard. A wrong pick costs 3 seconds.
  4. 10 points per right pick, plus up to 5 points for every second left when you finish (scaled by how many you got right).

Three dailies, the same for everyone, plus 1,000 levels of each difficulty. No mistakes for three stars, up to two for two.
